# spiralfiteval leaderboard

21 published spiral fits from 2 authors (Armando Gaona, Jashann), all scored by `tools/spiralfiteval` at commit `0f790d7` of my private working history (row commit ids do not resolve in this repository; see [README.md](README.md#provenance-of-a-row)). Generated by `build_table.py`; do not edit by hand.

**Read the floor column first.** A metric marked *at floor* does not beat what the same references score when they are moved off their sheet, so it says nothing about that fit and must not be read as a rank. The rule and the metric definitions are in [README.md](README.md). `held-out H1 = no` means the fit was given the human winding annotations as an input.

Lower is better for every column except H1 exact. `-` means the metric needs references that are not public for that scroll (H5 needs none); `n/a` in the floor column means the row has no reference metric that could sit at a floor.

Two caveats that apply to H5 on every row: `fit_spiral.py`'s default of 130 windings exports surplus windings outside the papyrus (villa PR #2048), and that is where almost all of the Paris 4 duplicates sit, so part of a fit's duplicate share is the default and not the fit; and a fit's own duplicates are invisible to the ink-pixel objective it was tuned on (villa PR #1842). Per-winding duplicate shares are in each row's JSON.
